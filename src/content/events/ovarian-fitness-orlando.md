Ovarian Fitness Orlando is a fitness fundraiser for the Ovarian Cancer Research Alliance (OCRA). Pick a class, fundraise with your team, and spend the day with people who get it.

Toteally Sisters fields a team every year. The 2026 goal was $20,000.
