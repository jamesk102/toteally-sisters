# Surface brief: Home (and site-wide shell)

Mode: Persuade (gentle). Build path: code-led (no image generation). Refinement of the incumbent Toteally Sisters identity, not a rebrand.

Audience and job: a woman facing gynecologic cancer (any type, any stage) or someone who loves her, usually on a phone and often tired or frightened. She leaves knowing the community is free, real and survivor-run, that she belongs whatever her diagnosis, and how to join. Action: Join a meetup, which goes to /join (the intake form); a team member then emails a hello and the Zoom link. Proof: real faces (Zoom collage, #nationalwearetealday collage, event photos), founders' names and stories, the next meetup always in view. No invented testimonials, counts or medical claims. Donations always route through OCRA.

Unresolved: meetup day, time and host (fall back to "monthly on Zoom, details emailed when you join"); upcoming events (all current ones have passed); the form recipient email and Resend key; Privacy and Terms text (drafted, pending owner review).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is the community's corkboard: real moments pinned up by the sisters themselves, with the next meetup pinned on top as an invitation you can accept. It refuses the category default of a nonprofit split hero (stock photo, mission statement, three icon cards, donate button).

OWN-WORLD: the pink ribbon-pattern paper (#F6DDEE ground, teal ribbon motif) is the board. Prints are white-bordered real photos at slight angles, held by translucent teal or pink tape. The invitation is a peach (#FFDCBC) card with a purple pin. Instrument Serif headlines with an italic purple accent word, Satoshi body. Purple #673B98 is reserved for actions. The black header carries the sticker logo. Shadows are neutral and offset. No eyebrow labels, no icon-card rows.

STORY: she sees women like her and reads "You don't have to do this alone." She learns it's free, survivor-founded and monthly on Zoom, and that her cancer type and caregivers count. She sees exactly three easy steps, then taps Join a meetup.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440x900): black header (logo left; Our Story, Monthly Meetup, Events, small "Donate via OCRA ↗", purple Join a meetup pill). Left column, 64px gutter: H1 at about 96px over two or three lines; a 21px lede; a row of welcome chips (Ovarian, Uterine, Cervical, Vulvar, In treatment or after, Caregivers). Right half: four taped prints (founders pair, Zoom collage, fountain group, wave) around a peach invitation card holding the next meetup and the primary Join button plus a "What to expect" link. Bottom-left: a 1-2-3 "how joining works" strip with large serif numerals. Phone: H1, lede, one taped print, the invitation card, chips, steps; a fixed bottom Join bar after the hero scrolls away. Signature interaction: on first load the prints settle onto the board once, with a slight drop and rotate into place, staggered, ease-out, under 700ms in total. Static under reduced motion.

FORM: "The community board", position 4 on my ordered list of 7 grounded home structures (dealt lead); seed:5b6cb7899535be50. Raises kept from declined challengers: a numbered 1-2-3 join sequence (brick instructions), one obvious next step closing every section (Game Boy), the next meetup always visible (algorave), pink and peach owning whole sections (rebel menu), an empty event list framed as an invitation (drum machine), truthful place and date tags on event prints (moon bazaar).
